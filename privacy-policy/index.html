Cell Stimulation Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: January 1, 2026 Platform: iOS Mobile Application

1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and practices of Cell Stimulation (“we,” “our,” or “us”) regarding the collection, use, handling, and disclosure of personal information when you access or use the Cell Stimulation mobile application (the “App”) and associated services (collectively, the “Platform”).

Cell Stimulation is a private digital platform designed to help members connect with their wellness coaching team, follow personalized wellness plans, complete check-ins, record journals, and communicate directly with their coaches. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

Advertising & Sale of Data: Cell Stimulation does not sell personal information. We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising, and we do not track you across third-party websites or applications for commercial marketing.

2. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of January 1, 2026. It applies to all current members, registered coaches, and individuals using the Cell Stimulation mobile application.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide the features and services available through the Platform. Depending on how you interact with the App, the information collected falls into the categories outlined in the following sections.

4. Account Information

To create an account and authenticate access to the Platform, we may collect:

  • Name: To identify you within the App and facilitate communication with your coaching team.
  • Email Address: Used for account authentication, access credentialing, important service notifications, and support inquiries.
  • Phone Number (Optional): If provided by your coaching team or yourself for communication or verification purposes.
  • User Identifier (User ID): A unique system-generated identifier used to manage your account and isolate your records.

5. Health and Wellness Information

As part of using the App's wellness coaching features, you or your coaching team may record lifestyle and self-reflection information, including:

  • Daily Check-Ins: Self-reported indicators of energy, rest quality, stress reflections, hydration notes, or daily routine ratings.
  • Wellness Goals & Milestones: Habit goals established in coordination with your coach (e.g. morning routines, breathing practices, evening wind-down habits).
  • Protocols & Guidance: Personalized lifestyle protocols and wellness recommendations assigned by your coaching team.

Important Notice: Cell Stimulation is a wellness coaching platform. It is not an electronic medical records (EMR) system, nor does it diagnose, cure, or treat medical diseases.

6. Communications and User Content

When you use the communication and journaling features of the App, we store:

  • Messages: Direct asynchronous text messages exchanged between you and your wellness coaching team.
  • Journal Entries: Personal reflection entries you write inside the App's journal tool. You can designate journal entries as private or share them with your coach.
  • Support Inquiries: Any questions, feedback, or problem descriptions sent directly to our support team.

7. Audio Data

If you or your coach utilize the voice note feature in messaging or journal dictation, the App accesses the device microphone with your explicit permission to record and transmit voice notes. Voice recordings are retained only to provide communication playback between you and your coach.

8. Photos and Videos

If the App enables profile picture customization or photo sharing with your coach, the App accesses your device camera or photo library only after you grant permission. Media files uploaded by members or coaches are used solely to display profile avatars or share coaching materials.

9. Device and Technical Information

When you use the mobile application, we may collect limited technical diagnostic information to maintain platform stability and troubleshoot errors:

  • Device Attributes: Device model, operating system version (e.g., iOS 17.x), screen resolution, and system language.
  • Performance & Crash Logs: Diagnostic data regarding application errors or crashes, used exclusively to fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Network & Connection Status: Basic internet connectivity indicators to ensure data synchronizes correctly.

10. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. Authenticate Users: Verify identity and maintain secure access to member accounts.
  2. Provide App Functionality: Deliver daily check-ins, habit tracking, journaling, and resource libraries.
  3. Connect Members with Coaching Teams: Enable coaches to review member progress, deliver guidance, and hold sessions.
  4. Provide Personalized Resources: Allow coaches to tailor wellness protocols and guides to member needs.
  5. Enable Communication: Facilitate in-app messaging and voice notes between members and practitioners.
  6. Maintain Security: Protect against unauthorized access, verify account integrity, and prevent fraudulent activity.
  7. Maintain and Improve the Service: Debug crashes, address technical issues, and improve platform responsiveness.

11. How We Share Information

Cell Stimulation does not sell, rent, or lease personal information to third parties. We share information only in the limited circumstances described below:

  • With Your Assigned Coaching Team: The primary purpose of the Platform is connecting you with your wellness coaches. Information you submit—such as check-ins, shared journal entries, and messages—is shared with the coaches assigned to your account.
  • With Trusted Service Providers: Subcontracted technical vendors (e.g., cloud hosting and database infrastructure) that support our operations under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • For Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, court subpoena, or governmental regulation to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

12. Service Providers

We rely on trusted cloud infrastructure providers to host and operate the platform securely:

  • Cloud Hosting & Authentication: Cloud database, storage, and authentication services (such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage by Google Cloud) are used to authenticate users, store structured application data, and host media files. These services process data on our behalf in accordance with strict security standards.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Crash reporting tools may be used to identify application faults and improve stability without collecting marketing profiles.

13. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide coaching services through the Platform. When you or your coach close or delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal records, subject to any legal obligations requiring temporary record retention.

14. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to member information is restricted to authorized personnel and the specific coaching team assigned to the member.

15. User Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access: The right to request a copy of the personal information we maintain about you.
  • Correction: The right to update or correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: The right to request the permanent deletion of your account and personal records.
  • Opt-Out: The right to withdraw consent for specific optional features such as microphone or photo library access via your iOS Settings.

16. Account Deletion

In compliance with Apple App Store guidelines, members have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time:

  1. How to Request Deletion: Send an email from your registered account email address to support@cellstimulation.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Verification: To protect your privacy and ensure security, our support team may request additional identity verification before processing the request.
  3. Processing & Removal: Upon verification, your account, check-in history, messages, journal entries, and personal profile information will be permanently removed from our active databases.

17. Children's Privacy

The Cell Stimulation platform is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.

18. International Users

If you access the Platform from outside the United States, please note that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in cloud data centers located in the United States and other jurisdictions where our cloud infrastructure providers maintain operations.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our platform features, legal requirements, or operational practices. When updates are published, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our data handling practices.
