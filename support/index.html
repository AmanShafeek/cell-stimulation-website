Faster Resolution

When Reporting a Problem

To help our engineering and support team resolve issues promptly, including the following optional details in your message is helpful:

Device Information:
Your iPhone or iPad model (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4).
App Version:
Located in the app Settings screen (e.g. Version 1.0).
Screenshots:
Attach an image or short recording showing what you experienced.
Response time: Inquiries are typically reviewed within 1–2 business days.

Quick answers to common questions about using the Cell Stimulation mobile application.
